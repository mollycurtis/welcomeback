# Asset prompts (Finn the Fish)

**Status (Oct 21 2026):** everything needed is in and built. Optional: a whale, a dolphin and a stingray for the sea quiz (prompt 7). The starfish and clam already exist in the game as Sandy and Pearlina.

For every prompt, attach one image from the project as a style reference so the new art matches:
for the map and the three backgrounds, a scene such as `public/finn/scenes/coral-gardens.jpg`;
for the shell, the fish and the sea creatures, a character such as
`public/finn/characters/bubbles-idle.png`. Make each sprite in its own chat. Backgrounds come
back as 1536 x 1024 images; sprites as 1024 x 1024 PNGs with a transparent background.

## 1. Travel map
Use the attached image only as a style reference: match its painted, bright, kid friendly cartoon style, soft shading and rich colors exactly.

Create a treasure map of an underwater world for a children's point and click game, seen from above, 1536 x 1024 pixels. The map is painted on old paper worn soft by the sea, with gently curled edges, in warm parchment tones with watercolor blues and greens for the water.

Seven small illustrated areas sit on the map, joined in this order by a dotted swimming path:
1. Top left: a town plaza with a giant open seashell on a little stage, beside an orange house with a blue door.
2. A busy harbor town: market stalls with striped awnings, wooden docks with a small red and white boat, and a stage shaped like a shell.
3. A garden: a forest of very tall kelp, a flower garden with a little stone fountain and a flower arch, and a hidden grotto with a waterfall and a glowing pool.
4. A deep trench: dark cliffs dropping into shadow, a tunnel lit by one lantern, and a cave full of glowing crystals.
5. A reef: a big green bronze gate, old coral ruins, and a small stone shrine.
6. A wooden sailing ship lying on its side on the sea floor.
7. Bottom right: a small island at night under a full moon, with festival lanterns glowing.

Leave clear empty space around each area for a label to be added later. Add a small compass rose with no letters on it. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers or labels anywhere in the image. Bright, friendly, and easy to read when small.

## 2. Shell game background
Use the attached image only as a style reference: match its painted, bright, kid friendly cartoon style exactly.

Paint a close up underwater scene for a children's game, 1536 x 1024 pixels: a flat, smooth, sandy colored rock that works as a table, seen from slightly above, filling the lower middle of the picture. Behind it, softly out of focus, a cheerful underwater market with striped awnings, hanging lanterns and rising bubbles, in warm afternoon light. The tabletop is completely empty: no shells, no pearls, no characters, no text.

## 3. Shell (for the shell game)
Use the attached image only as a style reference: match its painted cartoon style, outlines and soft shading exactly.

Draw a single seashell for a children's shell game: a pale pink and cream scallop shell, closed, seen from the side and standing upright like an upside down cup, so it could be lifted to show something underneath. Centered, filling about 70% of a square 1024 x 1024 canvas. Transparent background (PNG with alpha). No shadow, no ground, no text.

## 4. Counting game background
Use the attached image only as a style reference: match its painted, bright, kid friendly cartoon style exactly.

Paint a simple, calm underwater scene for a children's counting game, 1536 x 1024 pixels: open blue water with soft rays of light coming down from the surface, and a sandy sea floor along the bottom with a few clumps of coral and seaweed in the bottom corners and at the sides. Keep the whole middle of the picture empty, open water, because fish will swim across it. Bright and simple, not busy. No characters, no fish, no text.

## 5. Fish (four, one per chat)
Use the attached image only as a style reference: match its painted cartoon style, outlines and soft shading exactly.

Draw one small, friendly cartoon fish for a children's counting game, seen from the side and facing right, with big happy eyes and a small smile. Make it a bright yellow fish. Centered, filling about 70% of a square 1024 x 1024 canvas. Transparent background (PNG with alpha). No shadow, no bubbles, no text.

Then the same prompt three more times, changing the color: a purple fish; a green fish; an orange fish with white stripes.

## 6. Sea quiz background
Use the attached image only as a style reference: match its painted, bright, kid friendly cartoon style exactly.

Paint the inside of an underwater crystal cave for a children's quiz game, 1536 x 1024 pixels. In the middle stands a big chalkboard in a driftwood frame, completely blank, with no writing or chalk marks at all. Around it, softly glowing blue and purple crystals on the cave walls, and a small stack of old books and a little brass telescope in the bottom corners. Warm, curious and friendly. No characters, no text.

## 7. Sea creatures (four to six, one per chat)
Use the attached image only as a style reference: match its painted cartoon style, outlines and soft shading exactly.

Draw one friendly cartoon sea creature for a children's sea quiz: a seahorse. Full body, simple and easy to recognize, with a happy face. Centered, filling about 70% of a square 1024 x 1024 canvas. Transparent background (PNG with alpha). No shadow, no text.

Then the same prompt for each of: a whale; a dolphin; a stingray.
